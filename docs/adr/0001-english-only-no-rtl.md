# English only, no Arabic or RTL

noon.com UAE is bilingual (English and Arabic, with right-to-left layout), but Kart ships in English only, with no Arabic content and no RTL layout, for the MVP and the foreseeable future. Bilingual content would double every piece of Product content and every Seller form, and RTL touches every UI component. Neither teaches anything about the marketplace domain, which is the point of the project.

## Consequences

Adding Arabic later means retrofitting RTL across the whole UI and adding a second language for all Product content. That cost is accepted deliberately.
